# Write a decision with its source

Use this when someone will need to explain a choice after the people who made it have moved on.

1. Give the record a title that states the choice. Add the date, status and scope.
2. Explain what led to it. Point to an observation, a document or dated confirmation from the person who made the call.
3. Say what would prompt a review. If the choice has already changed, link the old and new records.
4. Ask a reader which fact prompted the choice and where they can check it. If access to the source is restricted, say who can verify it.

The [printed-code decision](../examples/lantern/decisions/printed-code.md) links a ticket design choice to two failed scans. For your own record, copy the [decision template](../template/decisions/decision.md).
