Keep a decision after a RoleShuttle review

In the CSV export example, the project owner chose to export only selected orders so a support operator could check which records would leave the app. The notebook links that choice to its source and says when to revisit it.

With RoleShuttle, your chat can hand implementation and review to different models. When you accept a decision worth keeping, ask the chat to write it in the Fieldnotes notebook you choose.
